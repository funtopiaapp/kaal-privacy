Eka Panchang

Privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026 · Eka Panchang for iPhone and iPad · Funtopia App Company

Eka Panchang collects nothing. We don't collect, store, share or sell any information about you. There's no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings (your tradition, language, home and away locations, reminder choices and appearance) are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • People you add for birthdays, remembrance days, birth charts and matching (their names, dates, times and places) are stored only on your device, with your other settings, and never sent anywhere.
  • Bookmarks and favourites (festivals, muhurthams, a day's timings and the prayers you keep) are kept only on your device.
  • Every calculation runs on the device, using data bundled inside the app, including the list of cities and the prayer texts, even with no internet connection.
  • Location, only if you ask. If you choose to use your current location, Eka Panchang reads it once, to the nearest kilometre, to find the closest city. It keeps only that city, never your coordinates, and the location never leaves your device. You can always pick a city by name instead.
  • Widgets. So that the widgets and Live Activities can show today's panchangam, the app keeps a short summary of the coming days (the panchangam and festivals for your home and away cities) in the device's keychain, shared only with its own widgets. It never leaves the device.
  • Reminders (festival reminders, briefings and notes when a family member's dasha period changes) are local notifications scheduled on your device. No server is involved.

Your calendar and photos

If you choose to add festivals to your calendar, Eka Panchang asks for permission to add events. It only adds events; it does not read your calendar. If you choose to save a greeting card to Photos, it asks for permission to add that image; it does not read your photo library. What it adds belongs to those apps and stays there until you delete it.

What is deleted, and when

Deleting the app removes your settings and the people you added. The widget summary described above may remain in the keychain; it holds only that calendar summary and the names of your chosen cities.

Purchases

Lifetime Full Access, the Muhurtham Finder, Porutham and the free trial are handled by Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your name, card or account details. The app asks the App Store only which purchases belong to your account.

Sharing

A greeting card, a prayer or a PDF is shared only when you choose to share it, through your device's own share sheet, to the people or apps you pick. It is created on your device.

The web edition

The free web edition at funtopiaapp.github.io/kaal-web keeps your choices in your own browser's storage and computes everything in the browser. It sends nothing to us.

Children

Eka Panchang is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. A change would never apply to information collected before it, because none is collected.
