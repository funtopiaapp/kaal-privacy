Privacy policy

Effective 21 October 2026 · Kaal for iPhone · Funtopia App

Kaal collects nothing. We don't collect, store, share or sell any information about you. There's no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

  • Your settings (your tradition, language, home and away locations and reminder choices) are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • People you add for birthdays and remembrance days (their names, dates, times and places) are stored only on your device, with your other settings, and never sent anywhere.
  • Bookmarks you save (festivals, muhurthams and a day's timings) are kept only on your device and are cleared six months after you save them.
  • Every calculation runs on the device, using data bundled inside the app, including the list of cities, even with no internet connection.
  • Location, only if you ask. If you choose to use your current location, Kaal reads it once, to the nearest kilometre, to find the closest city. It keeps only that city, never your coordinates, and the location never leaves your iPhone. You can always pick a city by name instead.
  • Widgets. So that Kaal's widgets and Live Activities can show today's panchangam, the app keeps a short summary of the coming days (the panchangam and festivals for your home and away cities) in the device's keychain, shared only with its own widgets. It never leaves the device.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No server is involved.

Your calendar

If you choose to add festivals to your calendar, Kaal asks for permission to add events. It only adds events; it does not read your calendar. Events it adds belong to your calendar app and stay there until you delete them.

What is deleted, and when

Deleting the app removes your settings. The widget summary described above may remain in the keychain; it holds only that calendar summary and the names of your chosen cities.

Purchases

Lifetime Full Access is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your name, card or account details. The app asks the App Store only whether the unlock has been purchased on your account.

Sharing

A greeting card is shared only when you choose to share it, through your phone's own share sheet, to the people or apps you pick. The card is created on your phone.

Children

Kaal is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. A change would never apply to information collected before it, because none is collected.
