Support

Eka Panchang is the Hindu calendar of your own tradition, for families in India and across the world: Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, North Indian, Gujarati, Marathi, Bengali, Odia and Nepali, and Sri Lankan Tamil, Malaysian & Singapore Tamil, South African Tamil, Caribbean, Fiji and Mauritius. Questions

What is free, and what do the purchases add?

The calendar, today's panchangam, the festivals for your tradition and the Prayers are free, with no time limit. Lifetime Full Access, a one-time purchase, adds festival reminders and briefings, the vrat journal, every divisional chart with the family's dasha notes, more themes, calendar styles and Home arrangement, PDF sharing, festivals in your calendar app, widgets and Live Activities, and every greeting card design. The Muhurtham Finder, which finds traditionally favoured days and hours for a marriage, housewarming and other occasions, and Porutham, the ten poruthams for a couple, are separate one-time add-ons. There is no subscription and no advertising.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. You can try every paid feature free for 7 days. Eka Panchang offers it after you set up, and on any purchase screen. Nothing is charged when it ends: the paid features simply lock again until you buy them. The trial is tied to your Apple ID, so it can be taken once.

I bought something. How do I get it on a new device?

Open any purchase screen in Eka Panchang and choose Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Why is a festival on a different day from another calendar?

Eka Panchang decides each day by the tithi or nakshatra at sunrise where you are, and for festivals that depend on a time of day (such as midday or midnight), by whether the tithi holds at that time. Two places can therefore observe the same festival on different days, and calendars printed for another city can differ. Traditions also differ: Malayalam and Tamil calendars follow solar months, and North Indian calendars name months by the full moon. Each festival page explains how its date was set. Check that your tradition and location are right in Settings.

My family is in another city. Can I see both?

Yes. Add an Away location in Settings. Eka Panchang shows both places side by side and points out any day where the observance falls differently.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check that notifications are allowed for Eka Panchang in the Settings app, and that the reminders you want are switched on in Eka Panchang's Settings. Reminders are scheduled on the device a couple of weeks ahead and refreshed whenever you open the app, so opening it now and then keeps them current.

Can I change my tradition or language later?

Yes, any time, from the tradition and language buttons at the top of Home or in Settings. The whole app changes straight away.

Does Eka Panchang make predictions?

No. It shows the calendar as each tradition keeps it. It never tells you what will happen to you and never sells remedies.
