Kaal is a Hindu calendar for your own tradition: Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Gujarati, Marathi, Bengali, Odia or North Indian. It shows your month names, your new year and your festivals, with the full daily panchangam, all computed on your iPhone for where you are.

Questions

What is free, and what does the unlock add?

The calendar, today's panchangam and the festival dates for your tradition are free, with no time limit. A one-time purchase, Lifetime Full Access, adds festival stories and step-by-step guides, puja timings, birthdays and remembrance days by star or tithi, your vrats with the Ekadashi parana time, evening briefings and festival reminders, home and away side by side, widgets and Live Activities, calendar export and every greeting card design. The Muhurtham Finder, which finds favoured days and hours for a marriage, housewarming, vehicle purchase and other occasions, is a separate one-time add-on. There is no subscription and no advertising.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. You can try every paid feature, the Muhurtham Finder included, free for 7 days. Kaal offers it after you set up, and on any unlock screen. Nothing is charged when it ends: the paid features simply lock again until you buy them. The trial is tied to your Apple ID, so it can be taken once.

I bought the unlock. How do I get it on a new phone?

Open Settings in Kaal and choose Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Why is a festival on a different day from another calendar?

Kaal decides each day by the tithi or nakshatra at sunrise where you are, and for festivals that depend on a time of day (such as midday or midnight), by whether the tithi holds at that time. Two places can therefore observe the same festival on different days, and calendars printed for another city can differ. Traditions also differ: Malayalam and Tamil calendars follow solar months, and North Indian calendars name months by the full moon. Check that your tradition and location are right in Settings.

My family is in another city. Can I see both?

Yes. Add an Away location in Settings. Kaal shows both places side by side and points out any day where the observance falls differently.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check that notifications are allowed for Kaal in the iPhone's Settings app, and that the reminders you want are switched on in Kaal's Settings. Reminders are scheduled on the phone a couple of weeks ahead and refreshed whenever you open the app, so opening Kaal now and then keeps them current.

Can I change my tradition later?

Yes, any time, in Settings. The whole calendar changes to the new tradition straight away.
